Link Collector

A bookmark you tap on each page of a forum thread as you read it. It gathers the links people posted into one list you can export. Nothing leaves your phone.

On a computer, drag this to your bookmarks bar instead: Collect links

Set up once

Add the bookmark

iPhone (Safari)
  1. Tap Copy bookmark code above.
  2. Bookmark this page: Share → Add Bookmark, name it Collect links, save to Favourites.
  3. Open Bookmarks, tap Edit, tap Collect links, clear the address and paste.
Android (Chrome)
  1. Tap Copy bookmark code, then bookmark this page as Collect links.
  2. Edit that bookmark and paste over its URL.
  3. To use it, type Collect links in the address bar on a forum page and tap the bookmark suggestion.
Every time

Collect as you read

How it works
  1. Open the thread page you want to start from, such as the TMC Shipping Movements thread.
  2. Tap the bookmark. A panel shows the links posted on that page, with who posted them.
  3. Tap Next page →, then tap the bookmark again. Repeat as far as you like.
  4. Tap CSV or Copy whenever you want the list.
Good to know

Only links inside posts are collected, not the forum's menus, member names or attachments. Links in quotes are credited to the original poster where possible, and tapping the same page twice won't create duplicates.

The list is kept in your browser on the forum's site, so it builds up across pages and days. Private browsing or clearing site data wipes it, so export as you go.
